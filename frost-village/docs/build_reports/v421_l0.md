**In one paragraph.** The carrying rules ship as the plan wrote them and do what they say: raw porters never leave their
line (0 % in every run, was 15 %), the 창고 opens at village complete and is built at 25 instead of 33 minutes, 짐꾼 2
follows at once, the cannery gets fish from the pile (+13–28 % cans), the crew only grows while it is busy on real-use
jobs (86–93 % of its time), and the early game is untouched. In a fresh game that is a clear win: 25–35 min smoked meat
empty 57 → 20 %, fish / wheat piles lower, oven blocked 14 → 2 %, income at 25–40 min +10 %. In the late village it is
not: the plan's model assumed the smelter would process more once its raw porter stopped walking away, but in steady
state the base smelter already runs 70 a minute (starved 3 %); the raw that used to be exported by the raw porters now
waits for the crew's raw runs, which come last behind a crew 86–93 % busy on use jobs, so ore and meat piles are fuller
(census any-pile 91 → 96 %) and income is flat (Δ +0.1 point). Smoked meat is demand-capped in the late village
(LD18), so the 판매대 shares do not move; the meat consumer (meat cans) is the next step. Levers in §4.

## 4. Why the late-village gates miss, and the levers

- **The smelter.** Base steady state: 70.2 a minute, starved 3 % (the warm-120 baseline's 10 % starved was the crew still
  growing). L0 removes the last starvation (0 %) but cannot raise a station running at its pace; the remaining loss is
  output blocked (10–17 %): the ingots wait for the line's 짐꾼 and the crew's direct runs.
