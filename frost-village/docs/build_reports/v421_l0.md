**In one paragraph.** The carrying rules ship as the plan wrote them and do what they say: raw porters never leave their
line (0 % in every run, was 15 %), the 창고 opens at village complete and is built at 25 instead of 33 minutes, 짐꾼 2
follows at once, the cannery gets fish from the pile (+13–28 % cans), the crew only grows while it is busy on real-use
jobs (86–93 % of its time), and the early game is untouched. In a fresh game that is a clear win: 25–35 min smoked meat
empty 57 → 20 %, fish / wheat piles lower, oven blocked 14 → 2 %, income at 25–40 min +10 %. In the late village it is
not: the plan's model assumed the smelter would process more once its raw porter stopped walking away, but in steady
state the base smelter already runs 70 a minute (starved 3 %); the raw that used to be exported by the raw porters now
waits for the crew's raw runs, which come last behind a crew 86–93 % busy on use jobs, so ore and meat piles are fuller
(census any-pile 91 → 96 %) and income is flat (Δ +0.1 point). Smoked meat is demand-capped in the late village
(LD18), so the 판매대 shares do not move; the meat consumer (meat cans) is the next step. Levers in §4.

## 4. Why the late-village gates miss, and the levers

- **The smelter.** Base steady state: 70.2 a minute, starved 3 % (the warm-120 baseline's 10 % starved was the crew still
  growing). L0 removes the last starvation (0 %) but cannot raise a station running at its pace; the remaining loss is
  output blocked (10–17 %): the ingots wait for the line's 짐꾼 and the crew's direct runs.
- **Ore and meat piles.** Miners bring ≈ 80 ore a minute against a smelter maximum of 76; hunters more meat than the
  smoked-meat demand (LD18). The surplus must leave by export. v4.2 exported part of it with the raw porters (6–7 a minute,
  at the cost of starving their stations) and the crew's early raw runs (7.5 a minute steady). L0 forbids the first (LD2)
  and puts the second last (r2 [10]); with the crew 86–93 % on use jobs, crew raw runs fell to 1–2.7 a minute and raw to
  the dock 30 → 17–21 a minute. The plan's model expected the stations to absorb it; measured, they do not.
- **Cannery starved.** The link more than doubles the crew's fish into the cannery (6.7 → 17.2 a minute steady) and cans
  +13–28 %, but cans are demand-limited (the 잡화점 / supermarket), so the cannery alternates between starved and blocked.
- **Income.** The plan's v4.2.1 band (Δ +5–20) came from "automation +325–475 coins in run L"; in steady state the
  probes show coins created 2,581 → 2,562 (−0.7 %) and the census Δ +0.1 point: what the crew now carries more of
  (cans, fish to the cannery) is demand-limited, and the raw exports pay little. D2 / D2b add a model ≈ +36 coins a minute,
  below the census noise. Not below the measured base, so not a regression, but under the band.
- **판매대 empty (census).** Bread is short because wheat is (the farm, not the carrying); smoked meat because the
  smokehouse alternates; both unchanged from the base.

Levers, in the order I would try them (none applied; each needs the same measurements again):
1. **Crew raw run before STORE-only overflow** for a pile full for 2 × `pileForS` whose station is at its pace (the
   touch's rule, but after the use jobs and the link). Expected to bring raw to the dock back to ≈ 25–30 a minute and the
   ore / meat piles back to the base; costs some 창고 filling with goods nobody buys.
2. `logistics.raw.stayOnLine: false` (balance only): restores the raw porters' spill; piles as in v4.2, raw porters
   away 11–15 % again, smelter starved 3–10 % again; all other L0 rules stay.
3. The planned consumers: meat cans (next), the 자재창고 + its export (L1a), the stall / feast / stove (L1b), 용광로 3단.
