## Full description (max 4000)

```text
Become the chief of Frost Village, a little village under the snow!

Chop wood, grill fish and carry bread as you and your neighbours grow a small camp into a busy town. Feel the goods stack up on the carrier on your back, pick up the coins your customers leave, and watch the village's days fill with new faces — at your own pace.

■ Carry, sell, grow
· Logs to the sawmill, fish to the grill! The chief carries the goods and keeps the shelves full.
· Wheat fields, a mine, a forge, a smokehouse, a cannery, a general store, a warehouse… every new shop means more to do and more coins.
· Hire porters and workers and the village slowly starts to run by itself.
· Raise watchtowers to clear the fog and open new land, then build houses, shops and decorations on the empty plots.
· Pick something up and the guide shows you where it goes.

■ The train brings the neighbour town
· Repair Frost Station and the train arrives with visitors from the next town.
· Fill regular orders, load them on the train, and help the neighbour town open its shops one by one.
· When the village grows, everyone gathers in front of the Town Hall for a rank-up celebration.

■ A busy restaurant
· Serve set meals of grilled fish, bread and smoked meat.
· Hire a cook and servers, add more tables, and welcome the lunch rush.

■ Your office, Frost Bank and Frost Depot
· In the chief's office you and your staff keep up with village news, letters and stock.
· Put spare coins in the bank; the depot and the cargo sleigh carry spare goods away.

■ A living village
· Every resident has a name and a personality. They have snowball fights, play tag and stop for a chat.
· Whistle for Kongi the dog, give him a pat and play with him.
· Talk to the residents: they answer in the village's own way and remember what you talked about.
· The residents chatter in a cute little village language of their own.
· Day turns to night, and street lamps and windows light up one by one.
· The sea rolls and the fishing boat cuts through the waves to bring in the catch.

■ Watch mode
· Put the phone down for a moment and the chief goes about his day on his own, with the camera following him around the village.

■ Good to know
· No login or sign-up.
· No ads and no in-app purchases.
· Plays without an internet connection.
· Your progress is saved automatically on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes the saved game too.
· Korean and English.
```
