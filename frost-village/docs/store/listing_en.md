## Full description (max 4000)

```text
Come and visit Frost Village, a little world of your own.

A small village by a snowy sea. It starts as a tiny camp, but every time the chief carries goods and opens a new shop, new neighbours move in, and at night more windows glow. Every resident has a name and a personality: they work, sit down for a meal, throw snowballs and stop for a chat, each living their own day. There is no rush. Just watch your village grow, at your own pace.

■ A village that grows little by little
· Logs to the sawmill, fish to the grill. The chief carries a full load on his back and keeps the shops stocked.
· Wheat fields, a mine, a forge, a smokehouse, a cannery, a general store, a warehouse… every new shop brings new faces.
· Hire workers and porters and the village slowly starts to run by itself.
· Raise watchtowers to clear the fog and open new land, then build houses, shops and decorations on the empty plots.
· When the village grows, everyone gathers in front of the Town Hall to celebrate together.

■ Neighbours living their own days
· Snowball fights, games of tag, little chats in the square. Nobody tells them to; they just live their day.
· At lunchtime everyone meets at the big restaurant for a warm meal. Hire a cook and servers and add more tables.
· Talk to the residents: they answer in the village's own way and remember what you talked about.
· The residents chatter in a cute little village language of their own.
· Whistle for Nuri the little Maltipoo, give her a pat and play together.

■ The train to the next town
· Repair Frost Station and the train brings visitors over from the neighbour town.
· Send regular orders on the train and watch the neighbour town open its shops one by one.

■ Sea and sky
· Day slowly turns to night. When the sun goes down, street lamps and windows light up one by one.
· The sea rolls and the fishing boat comes home through the waves.

■ Just sit back and watch: Watch mode
· Put the phone down for a while and the chief spends the day on his own, with the camera following him around the village.

■ The chief's little office
· Keep up with village news and letters together with your staff.
· Spare coins go to Frost Bank; spare goods go to the depot and the cargo sleigh.

■ For you if you
· want a few quiet minutes at the end of the day
· love watching a small village grow
· like snowy winter scenery and cute villagers
· want a game to play offline in spare moments

■ Good to know
· No login or sign-up.
· No ads and no in-app purchases.
· Plays without an internet connection.
· Your progress is saved automatically on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes the saved game too.
· Korean and English.
```
