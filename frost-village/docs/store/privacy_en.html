Privacy Policy

Nuri Entertainment (누리엔터테인먼트) ("we") makes and publishes the mobile game "Snowbloom Village" (행복한 눈꽃마을 이야기; Android app, package name com.snowbloom.village; "the game").
Effective date: 27 October 2026

In short
  • We do not collect any personal information. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no purchases.

1. Information we collect

None. The game does not collect your name, email address, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos or files, or any device identifier (including the advertising ID). There is no way to create an account or sign in.

2. What is stored on your phone only

So that you can continue where you left off, the game keeps the following in the app's own storage on your phone. It never leaves the phone and we cannot see it.

WhatWhy
Your game progress (coins, buildings, residents, progress steps) and a backup copy of itTo continue the game next time
Settings (sound, music, language, graphics, play options)To remember your choices
Title screen state (whether you saw the intro)Not to show the intro every time
"Chat with residents": the last few lines shown in the chat window, what the residents remember and how close they feel to youSo residents remember earlier talks

Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Apps → Snowbloom Village (행복한 눈꽃마을 이야기) → Storage → Clear data, deletes all of it. We keep no copy, so we cannot restore it. (If Android backup, section 5, was on, Android may restore its own backup copy when you reinstall with the same Google account.)

Moving your village. Settings → "Move village → Export" creates a text code of your game progress on the phone. The code leaves the phone only if you copy it or share it to another app yourself (notes, messengers); it is never sent to us. It contains your game progress, so please do not send it to other people.

3. What you type in "Chat with residents"

When you type to a resident, the text is read on your phone only to pick a reply in the village's own voice from lines built into the game. It is not sent over the internet or to any outside service (including AI services). The last few lines of the chat are stored with your progress (section 2). Real-life details such as school, family or age are left out of what residents remember for longer, but please do not type personal information such as your name, phone number or address in the first place.

4. Internet and permissions

All pictures, sounds and code of the game are inside the app, so it plays without an internet connection. The game does not send or receive any information over the internet. The app asks for two permissions only, both normal permissions granted at install:

PermissionUsed for
Vibrate (VIBRATE)Short vibration effects when you gather goods, collect coins or unlock a building or a worker. Turning the sound off in the game's settings also turns vibration off.
Internet (INTERNET)A technical requirement of the Android system component that draws the game (WebView). The game does not communicate through it, and it has no links that open web pages.

The app does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo/file access. The app also turns off WebView's Safe Browsing lookups and usage statistics.

5. Android backup on your phone

If Android backup (Google backup) is turned on on your phone, Android may copy this game's saved data, like other apps' data, to the backup in your own Google account. That backup is managed by Google under your device settings and Google's policies; we cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

6. Ads, analytics and third parties

The game has no ads and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or other third-party SDKs. There are no in-app purchases. We therefore do not share personal information with, or have it processed by, any third party.

7. Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. You can delete the data on your phone at any time as described in section 2. If you have questions, contact us below.
